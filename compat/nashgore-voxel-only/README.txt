TuinDoom RT - NashGore voxel-only compatibility pack

Keeps NashGore objects that have converted RT voxel replacements:
- physical gibs
- crushed remains
- squished remains
- frozen remains

Suppresses NashGore sprite-only effects that have no converted voxel model:
- blood drops and impact sprays
- liquid/pool effects
- trails and footprints
- wall/floor splats

This pack must load after nashgore.pk3.
