# TuinDOOM RT 1.4.7.8

This release adds realistic sky and lighting presets across Doom and Doom II, and improves the launcher based on player feedback.

## Changes

- Added optional realistic sky and directional-light presets for Doom Episodes 1–3.
- Added three chapter-specific realistic sky and lighting presets covering Doom II MAP01–32.
- Added six curated light directions per preset, cycled with `N`.
- Reworked the launcher with randomized Doom sky artwork and a cleaner, compact layout.
- Added selectable blood effects: fluids with decals, fluids only, decals only, or neither.
- Enabled menu mouse control by default.
- Restored the colored-sky-lighting option for original and custom skies under a clearer label.
- Added a warning that Frame Generation may distort HUD and weapon sprites.
- Fixed visible sky seams and improved sunlight balance in the realistic presets.

## Requirements

- Windows 10/11 64-bit.
- An RTX/Vulkan-capable GPU with current drivers.
- A legally owned Doom or Doom II IWAD.
